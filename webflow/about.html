How we work together

1. Discovery Meeting

Our discovery meeting is all about getting to know you, understanding your business and goals. No commitments, just a chance for us to connect, share ideas, and see if we're a good fit for each other. One thing to know about me: I'm very straight forward and I will never recommend something you don't need!

2. Onboarding

Welcome aboard! Now that we've decided to work together, our onboarding process is designed to make things smooth and effortless for you. We'll gather all the necessary details, set up communication channels, and ensure you have access to me and the team who is ready to turn your ideas into reality.

3. Strategy and Development

We'll collaborate closely to develop a customised strategy or plan of action that aligns with your goals. Whether it's graphic and web design, email marketing, or front-end web development, we'll map out a plan that captures your brand needs and meets your objectives. This is where creativity meets strategy, and we're excited to bring your vision to life.

4. Feedback Rounds

Your input matters! We value your thoughts and ideas, and this step is dedicated to ensuring the work reflects your vision. We'll present drafts, designs, and strategies for your review, and your feedback will guide us in refining and perfecting the details. It's a collaborative process, and we're committed to making sure the final result exceeds your expectations.

5. Project Delivery

With your feedback incorporated and every detail polished, we're thrilled to deliver the final product. Whether it's a stunning website, captivating graphics, or a well-executed marketing strategy, this is the culmination of our joint efforts. We will still be here to support you whenever you need after your project is delivered.
