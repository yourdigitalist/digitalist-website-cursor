Privacy Policy

This policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our website or services.

Information we collect

We may collect personal information, including but not limited to, your name, email address, phone number, and billing information when you sign up for our newsletter, fill out any form, book a meeting, or otherwise provide us with your personal information. We also collect non-personal information such as browser type, device information, and website usage data.

How we use your information

We use your personal information to provide and improve our services, process payments, respond to enquires, send resources, newsletters and communicate with you about our services. We may also use non-personal information for analytics and to enhance user experience. We may also use cookies or similar technologies to collect information about your browsing behaviour and to serve personalised advertisements on our website and on other websites you visit.

Information sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website or conducting our business, provided they agree to keep the information confidential.

How we secure information

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You have the right to access, correct, update, or delete your personal information. You can also opt-out of receiving promotional communications from us by following the instructions provided in the communication.

Changes to this policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with a revised date.

Contact us

If you have any questions or concerns about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at marina@yourdigitalist.com. By using our website or services, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.
